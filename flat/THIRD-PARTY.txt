Sepang centerline and left/right widths:
TUMFTM/racetrack-database, tracks/Sepang.csv
https://github.com/TUMFTM/racetrack-database
The repository declares GNU LGPL v3; its LICENSE and original Sepang CSV are included.
Source coordinates came from OpenStreetMap and widths from satellite images;
see the repository README for its processing and accuracy limitations.

Original horizontal alignment reference:
User-supplied Sepang GLB from the original banked/elevated project.
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sepang-international-circuit-2025-layout-590bf243480e43f18e588a3908daa4df
The flat driving surface does not use the mesh elevation or banking.
The original mesh/textures are not included. A frozen horizontal-layout reference,
source hashes and transformations are recorded in source/sepang-reference.json
and generation-report.json.

Model-derived kerb and runoff reference:
source/sepang-kerbs.json records red/white kerb strip geometry/colour evidence
from Sepang101Mtl and gravel/painted runoff bands from Sepang1641Mtl and
Sepang1631Mtl in the same user-supplied GLB. Sepang1401Mtl provides the local
driving-strip boundary for side detection and runoff widths. The original
textures and mesh are not redistributed. This is an approximate adaptation
to a wider flat layout, not surveyed real-world geometry; see surface-reference.md.

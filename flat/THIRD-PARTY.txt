Sepang centerline and left/right widths:
TUMFTM/racetrack-database, tracks/Sepang.csv
https://github.com/TUMFTM/racetrack-database
The repository declares GNU LGPL v3; its LICENSE and original Sepang CSV are included.
Source coordinates came from OpenStreetMap and widths from satellite images;
see the repository README for its processing and accuracy limitations.
